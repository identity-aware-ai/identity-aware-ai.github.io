# Accepted Papers

Accepted papers for Identity-Aware AI 2026 will be announced after the notification date on March 20, 2026.

## Camera-Ready Submission Guidelines

**Note:** These guidelines will be updated once papers are accepted.

- __Deadline__: Camera-ready submissions are due by March 30, 2026
- __Format__: All accepted papers must follow the LREC 2026 formatting guidelines available at: [https://lrec2026.info/authors-kit/](https://lrec2026.info/authors-kit/)
- __What to upload__: Submit the following as your camera-ready package:
  - Final paper PDF
  - Signed author agreement (if required by LREC 2026)
- __Checklist before uploading__:
  - Verify authors, affiliations, title, and abstract are final and correct
  - Ensure the paper compiles without errors and all fonts are embedded in the PDF
  - Confirm figures/tables are legible and references are resolved
  - Include ethics and limitations sections

If you have questions about the formatting requirements, please refer to the official LREC 2026 instructions. For any workshop-specific questions, contact the organizers at [identity-aware-ai@googlegroups.com](mailto:identity-aware-ai@googlegroups.com).
